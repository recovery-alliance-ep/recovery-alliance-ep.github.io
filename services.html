Programs & services

How we can help

Recovery Alliance offers multiple pathways — each program is described in full on its own page. Start wherever you are.

All programs

Peer-led support across every stage of recovery.

Each program below links to a dedicated page with the full description. We never repeat the same content twice.

One-to-one

Peer Recovery Coaching

One-to-one support from state-certified peers who have navigated recovery themselves. Available to individuals and families.

Learn more
Harm reduction & outreach

Punto de Partida

A low-barrier drop-in center with basic needs support — showers, laundry, clothing, food, and a respite area — plus street outreach.

Learn more
Overdose prevention

Region 10 Naloxone Distribution Hub

Recovery Alliance is the Region 10 naloxone (Narcan) resource hub. Pick up for free — no ID, no referral, no judgment. Training available for individuals, families, and organizations.

Get free naloxone
Residential

Casa Vida de Salud

A structured residential recovery home in central El Paso serving adults 18+. Recovery coaching, life skills, and connection to treatment and MOUD providers.

Learn more
Women's residential

Alas Nuevas

A residential program for women in recovery. Peer-led support, life skills, and connection to community.

Learn more
Justice-involved

Re-entry Program

Supports people leaving jail or detention in returning to the community — housing, employment, family reunification, and connection to treatment.

Learn more
DWI

DWI Program

Peer support and recovery resources for people navigating DWI-related requirements and recovery.

Learn more
Navigation

Linkage to Treatment & MOUD
