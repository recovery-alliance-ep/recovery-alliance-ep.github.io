Residential recovery home

Casa Vida de Salud

A structured residential home in central El Paso where adults in recovery build skills, community, and a path forward.

What it is

A home built for recovery.

Casa Vida de Salud opened February 14, 2005 and is located in central El Paso. It is a structured residential recovery home that provides adults 18 and older with a stable, peer-supported environment while they work toward long-term recovery.

Who it serves

Adults 18 and older in recovery from substance use disorder.

Daily life

Residents follow a structured daily curriculum that includes:

  • Recovery coaching from Texas certified peer support specialists
  • Life skills groups
  • Education about substance use disorder and recovery
  • Spiritual support and introduction to 12-step and faith-based programs
  • Social and recreational events
  • Free Narcan (naloxone)

Support we connect you to

We connect residents to treatment providers and MOUD (medications for opioid use disorder) providers in the community. Casa Vida coordinates with external clinical and health resources — residents are supported in navigating the full system of care.

Multiple pathways in

Casa Vida accepts residents through multiple pathways, including self-referral.
