Residential recovery home

Casa Vida de Salud

A structured residential home in central El Paso where men in recovery build skills, community, and a path forward.

What it is

A home built for recovery.

Casa Vida de Salud opened February 14, 2005, in central El Paso. It is a structured residential recovery home that gives men 18 and older a stable, peer-supported place to live while they work toward long-term recovery.

Who it serves

Men 18 and older in recovery from substance use.

Program options

Casa Vida offers more than one path, so you can find the one that fits where you are.

  • 90-day residential program. A structured stay with daily recovery groups, peer coaching and life skills.
  • Project Homes (90 days). Our program for residents who take medication for opioid use disorder (MOUD, sometimes called MAT). You're welcome here.
  • Sober living. A next step after the residential program: supervised housing while you work or go to school, with continued meetings and peer coaching.

Daily life

Every day at Casa Vida includes:

  • Three meals a day
  • Three recovery groups a day
  • Outside NA and AA meetings, every day of the week

Residents also get:

  • Recovery coaching from Texas certified peer support specialists
  • Life skills groups
  • Education about substance use and recovery
  • Spiritual support and introduction to 12-step and faith-based programs
  • Social and recreational events
  • Free naloxone (Narcan)

Support we connect you to

We connect residents to treatment and MOUD providers in the community, including San Vicente, MedMark and Aliviane.

How to get in

You can refer yourself, or someone can refer you.

  1. Call us, starting at 8 a.m. (915) 594-7000
  2. Bring an ID if you have one. If you don't have one, that's OK.
  3. If a bed isn't open right away, you may be placed on the waitlist. The Casa Vida director will tell you about current availability, next steps, and how to follow up about admission.
