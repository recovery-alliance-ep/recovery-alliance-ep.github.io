Recovery Alliance of El Paso

You do not have to find your way alone.

Recovery Alliance is a peer-led Recovery Community Organization serving El Paso and West Texas. Our staff bring lived experience of substance use recovery, mental health recovery, and justice involvement—so support begins with understanding, dignity, and hope.

Accredited RCO

Experience, Strength and Hope.

Accredited by the Council on Accreditation of Peer Recovery Support Services (CAPRSS).

Recovery grows in community.

We are an accredited Recovery Community Organization: people in recovery, families, and allies building connection through peer-led services, social activities, and advocacy. Recovery Alliance fosters recovery as a life-affirming process and works to smash stigma and remove barriers.
