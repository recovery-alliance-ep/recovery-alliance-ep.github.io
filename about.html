Who we are

About Recovery Alliance of El Paso

A safe, nonjudgmental space where people affected by substance use disorder can find immediate help, pathways to healing, and hope — guided by people who have been there before.

Our mission

A community where long-term recovery thrives.

Recovery Alliance of El Paso is a safe, nonjudgmental space where individuals, families, and communities affected by substance and alcohol use disorder can find immediate help, pathways to healing, and hope — guided by people who have been there before.

We are a peer-led Recovery Community Organization. That means our staff bring lived experience of substance use recovery, mental health recovery, and justice involvement. Support begins with understanding, dignity, and hope. No stigma. No shame.

What we do today

Recovery Alliance provides peer-led services across several programs. Each is described in full on its own page:

  • Peer recovery coaching
  • Punto de Partida — drop-in center, street outreach, naloxone hub
  • Casa Vida de Salud — residential recovery home
  • Alas Nuevas — women's residential program
  • Re-entry program
  • DWI program

Our history

Over 25 years of peer-led recovery in El Paso.

1998

El Paso Alliance founded by people in recovery, families, and allies to open a public dialogue about addiction, treatment, and recovery — and to fight stigma.

March 1999

Community meetings begin.

April 11, 2000

City of El Paso proclaims El Paso Alliance Day of Sobriety.

2001

501(c)(3) status granted.

2002

Shift from community advocacy to direct peer services.

February 14, 2005

Casa Vida de Salud opens; serves 126 people in its first year.

2006 – present
