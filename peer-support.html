Peer Support & Recovery Coaching

Support from people who have been there — state-certified peers walking alongside you in recovery.

What is peer support?

Recovery support that starts with lived experience.

Peer support means being alongside someone who has navigated recovery from substance use disorder themselves. Recovery Alliance peer coaches are Texas state-certified and bring personal knowledge of what recovery looks, feels, and takes — no judgment, no shame.

Peer support complements professional care. It is not a substitute for therapy, clinical treatment, or emergency services. Peer coaches help you navigate the system, stay connected, and build your path forward.

What a peer coach can help with

  • Setting and working toward personal recovery goals
  • Finding and connecting to treatment and MOUD (medications for opioid use disorder) providers
  • Navigating housing, employment, and benefits paperwork
  • Rebuilding family relationships
  • Connecting to recovery community activities and social support
  • Free Narcan (naloxone) and overdose prevention education

Who we serve

Peer support services at Recovery Alliance are available to people in recovery from substance use disorder, their family members, and allies — in El Paso and across West Texas.

How it works

Getting started is straightforward.

  1. Reach out. Call (915) 594-7000, stop by in person, or send us a message.
  2. Talk with a peer. We'll learn about what you need and match you with the right peer coach.
  3. Build your path. Your peer coach walks alongside you — at whatever pace works for you.
