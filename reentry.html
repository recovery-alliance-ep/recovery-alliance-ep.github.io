Re-entry Program

Supporting people leaving jail or detention in returning to the community — with housing, employment, family, and recovery resources.

About this program

From incarceration to community.

Recovery Alliance of El Paso's re-entry program helps people leaving jail or detention return to the community and become self-supporting, with a goal of reducing recidivism. We address the real barriers people face when coming home.

What we address

  • Housing stability
  • Employment and job readiness
  • Family reunification
  • Connection to treatment for substance use disorder
  • Mental and physical health care

Coordination with jail-to-community systems

Our team coordinates with jail-to-community transition systems to support a smooth release and first steps back in the community, including:

  • Release orders
  • Housing assessment
  • Transportation
  • Medication continuity

Who it serves

People leaving jail or detention in the El Paso area.
