Your privacy

Privacy

You can read this whole website without telling us who you are. Here is what happens with your information, in plain words.

The short version

We don't track you.

  • You don't need an account or a login to use this site.
  • This site does not use advertising, tracking tools, or visitor analytics.
  • This site does not set cookies.
  • We do not sell or trade anyone's information.
  • We only see what you choose to send us.

When you send us a message

If you use the form on our Contact page, we receive what you type: your name, your phone number or email if you give one, and your message. We use it to get back to you. That is all we use it for.

The form is delivered to us by a service called Formspree, which passes your message along to Recovery Alliance staff.

Please don't put private health details in the form. A short note like "I'd like to talk to a peer" is enough. We can talk about the rest by phone or in person.

When you call or visit

This page is about the website. What you share with a peer by phone or in person is handled under Recovery Alliance's own confidentiality practices. If you have questions about that, ask any staff member.

What the website itself records

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may keep basic technical records of visits, such as a visitor's IP address, for security. Recovery Alliance does not receive or look at those records.

The fonts and images on this site are stored on the site itself, so viewing a page does not contact any other company.

There is one exception, and it is your choice. On our naloxone page, if you press "Show the map", the map pictures load from OpenStreetMap, a nonprofit map service. Nothing loads from them unless you press the button. The same locations are listed on the page in plain text.

Links to other websites

Some pages link to other websites, such as our partners or a map for directions. Those sites have their own privacy practices. Calling a number on this site uses your own phone service.

If you share a phone or computer

Your browser may remember that you visited this site. If you share a device and want to keep your visit private, you can use a private or incognito window, or clear your browsing history afterward.

Questions, or want your message deleted?

Call us at (915) 594-7000 and ask. If you sent us a message and want it removed, tell us and we will delete it.
