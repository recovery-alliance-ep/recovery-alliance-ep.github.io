Intensive Outpatient Program (IOP)

More support during the week, while you keep living at home.

About this program

A step up in support, without leaving home.

The Intensive Outpatient Program is for people who want more structure and more hours of support each week than a weekly group — while living at home and keeping up with work and family.

IOP is low to no cost.

How to get connected

Call us at (915) 594-7000 or send a message to learn more.
